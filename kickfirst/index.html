KickFirst

Making soccer practice something kids actually want to do.

My kid’s soccer coach wanted everyone practicing their juggling for ten minutes a day. Hardly anyone was doing it. I started building KickFirst to make practice more rewarding, with competition centered on consistency and improvement rather than raw ability.

Independent project · In development

Case study in progress

I’ll be sharing how I designed the practice experience, built a points system around improvement instead of ability, and handled shared family accounts and team management. KickFirst also became the testing ground for my agentic development workflow, which I later adapted for Wistia’s production codebase.
