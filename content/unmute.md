## Where it started

- Heading: Where it started
- Body:
  1. Around 2000, I uploaded my band’s music to GarageBand.com, an early music-review site (not the Apple app). To get your songs reviewed, you had to review other people’s first, without knowing who made them until you’d finished. I always thought that was clever.
  2. Years later, when I was trying to get better at mixing and couldn’t afford professional feedback, I started building Unmute around that same idea.
  3. As I learned more about software development, I kept using Unmute to try new technologies. I’d rather find a way to use them in something I cared about than build another todo app.
