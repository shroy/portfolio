# Story

Before software, I spent years making music.

I composed, performed, recorded and mixed.

## Music

Drums came first. I wrote and performed music for years, and I also worked as a recording and mixing engineer.

## Woodburning

I also woodburned art into drum shells. The tea party below is from an Alice in Wonderland shell I did in raw birch, and I made a raven for another drummer’s kit.

## Learning to build

When I started learning to code, I skipped the tutorial apps and went after bigger, original ideas.

[Unmute](/unmute/), a music feedback platform, is the one I kept coming back to. Each time I picked it up, it needed something I didn’t know yet, like audio handling, interaction design, frontend architecture or backend work.

## Today

At Novillus, [Provide](/provide/) and [Wistia](/wistia/) I’ve worked on backend architecture and realtime systems as much as product interfaces and design systems. On the side I’m still building things, lately [KickFirst](/kickfirst/), [HookTML](/#hooktml) and [Roleprint](/roleprint/).
